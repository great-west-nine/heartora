Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Heartora (“Heartora,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use the Heartora mobile application (the “App”), our related services, and this website (collectively, the “Services”). Heartora is an entertainment experience featuring fictional AI characters, AI-powered conversations, and AI-generated photos.

If you have a privacy question or would like to exercise a privacy right, email owner@great-west-nine.pro.

Information we may collect

The information we process depends on which features you use and how the App is configured. Categories may include:

  • Account information: identifiers, profile details, and sign-in information if you create an account or use an authentication feature.
  • Conversations and app activity: messages you send to AI characters, selected characters, and information needed to provide conversations and related features.
  • Images and entitlements: the AI-generated images you choose to view or unlock and records of your access to digital content.
  • Purchase information: in-app coin balances, transaction identifiers, purchase status, and entitlements. Payments through Apple are handled by Apple; we do not need your full payment card number to provide digital entitlements.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system, app version, approximate network information, IP address, diagnostics, and security or performance logs where collected.
  • Support communications: your email address and information you provide when contacting support.

We ask you not to include sensitive personal information in your chats unless it is necessary and you understand the privacy implications.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate and improve the Services; deliver AI-generated responses; maintain character experiences; display and unlock digital content; process and restore purchase entitlements; troubleshoot errors; answer support requests; prevent misuse; protect the security of the Services; and comply with legal obligations.

Where required by applicable law, processing is based on contractual necessity, legitimate interests, consent, or a legal obligation, depending on the activity. You may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis, subject to lawful processing already performed.

AI processing and service providers

To generate a character’s response, the content of your message and relevant conversation context may be transmitted to AI technology providers that process information on our behalf or otherwise provide the model infrastructure used by the App. Hosting, storage, diagnostics, security, customer-support, and payment-related providers may also process information as necessary to perform their services.

Some providers may be located outside your country. Where required, appropriate protections are used for international data transfers. The App should present any disclosures and consent choices required by applicable law before sharing personal data with third-party AI services.

In-app purchases

Heartora may offer virtual coins that can be used to unlock additional AI-generated photos. Purchases made through the iOS App are processed through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple’s handling of payment information is governed by Apple’s own policies. We may receive transaction confirmations and entitlement information required to deliver purchased digital items and support purchase restoration.

How long information is kept

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including operating an account, delivering requested features, handling purchase records and disputes, protecting the Services, and meeting legal obligations. Actual retention periods vary according to the information type and legal requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we take steps to delete or de-identify it, unless further retention is permitted or required by law. You can contact us to request deletion of information associated with your use of Heartora.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, portability of, or restriction or objection to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with a competent privacy authority.

To submit a privacy request, email owner@great-west-nine.pro with the subject “Heartora Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity to protect your information. We respond in accordance with applicable law.

Children and age requirements

Heartora is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. Individuals under 18 should not use the Services. If you believe a minor has provided personal information through Heartora, contact owner@great-west-nine.pro so the matter can be reviewed.

More information about child safety and reporting is available in our Children’s Safety Policy.

Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical steps intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No method of transmitting or storing information is perfectly secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Website technology and third-party links

This website is a static informational website. It does not include an account sign-in, advertising pixels, or a built-in contact form. Its design may load a web font from Google Fonts, and website hosting providers may collect routine access logs such as IP addresses and user-agent information. Following links to third-party services may subject you to their separate privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The updated version will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date. If a change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions, personal-data requests, or concerns about this Policy, contact the Heartora team at owner@great-west-nine.pro. You may also visit our Contact Us page.
