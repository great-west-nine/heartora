Privacy Policy

How Heartora uses anonymous identifiers, stores AI conversations, manages photo access, and responds to privacy requests.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Heartora ("Heartora," "we," "us," or "our") handles information through the Heartora mobile application (the "App") and the related website (together, the "Services"). Heartora provides conversations with fictional AI characters and access to AI-generated character images.

You can use the App without creating an account or providing a name, email address, or password. However, using Heartora is not entirely anonymous to our systems: a persistent, randomly assigned user identifier is used to store your conversations and in-app progress.

For privacy requests, please contact owner@great-west-nine.pro.

Information we collect

We process the following information when you use Heartora:

  • Anonymous user ID. The App uses a unique identifier assigned to your app profile. We use it to associate saved conversations, your free coin balance, and unlocked content with your use of the App. No email-based registration is required.
  • AI conversations. We store messages you send to AI characters and relevant conversation history on our server so the chat features can operate and conversations can be available during your use of the App.
  • AI photo interactions. We store records of AI-generated photos you have opened or unlocked and may associate them with your anonymous user ID.
  • Free virtual coins and content access. Our server keeps track of the virtual coins allocated to your anonymous profile and of coin use for unlocking in-app content.
  • Technical and service information. When the App connects to servers, basic connection information (which can include an IP address, timestamps, request information and server logs) may be processed by our hosting infrastructure to deliver the service, troubleshoot errors and maintain security. The exact content and retention of these logs depend on the service configuration.
  • Support messages. If you email our support team, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in that message. An email address is not required to chat in the App.

We do not use an app analytics service according to the current Heartora configuration. Stored photo unlocks are used to operate app features, not to imply that user activity is unrecorded.

Please avoid including passwords, financial information, identification documents, or other sensitive personal information in AI conversations.

Why we use information

  • To recognize the app's anonymous profile and maintain saved conversations.
  • To generate AI character responses and provide context when you continue a chat.
  • To display character photos and remember which images have been opened or unlocked.
  • To allocate and manage free virtual coins used inside the App.
  • To operate, protect, debug and maintain the App and its servers.
  • To respond to support or privacy requests and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Heartora is intended for entertainment. AI chat messages are generated by artificial intelligence rather than real people.

AI processing and service providers

When you send a message to an AI character, the text you submit and the relevant conversation context are processed to generate the reply. Depending on the technology used by Heartora, this processing may involve a third-party AI model or inference provider. These providers may receive chat text and relevant context when needed to perform that function.

We may also use infrastructure and hosting services to transmit and store the data required to operate the App. Service providers may operate in different countries; where applicable, cross-border transfers must have appropriate legal safeguards.

Whether an external AI provider retains prompts or uses them for model improvement depends on the provider and the service configuration. We do not claim in this Policy that conversations are never retained by external providers or never used for training without first verifying those practices.

When a third-party AI service receives personal data, its identity, the purpose of sharing, and any required permission must be disclosed through the App before the data is shared, in accordance with applicable rules. This Privacy Policy does not replace an in-app permission request where one is required.

Free virtual coins and AI photos

Heartora provides virtual coins for free through the App's server. Coins can be used to unlock additional AI-generated photos. The App does not offer purchases of coins or other in-app purchases for real money in the current version.

To make these features work, our server keeps the balance assigned to your anonymous ID and records which images have been unlocked. These records are app functionality data, not payment transaction records. The coins are not redeemable for cash.

Legal bases where applicable

If laws such as the GDPR apply, the legal basis for processing depends on the activity: providing the features you request, maintaining the security and reliability of the Services, complying with a legal obligation, or consent when required. Where we rely on legitimate interests, these are generally the secure operation and abuse prevention of the Services, balanced against your rights.

If consent is required for a particular use or transfer of your information, you should have the opportunity to make that choice before the processing occurs. You may withdraw consent when consent is the legal basis; this may prevent certain features from working.

Data retention and deletion

Conversation history, free coin balances and unlocked-image records are associated with your persistent anonymous ID and retained to preserve the related features and progress. They may remain on our servers after you close or uninstall the App; uninstalling the App alone does not necessarily delete those records.

We retain information while it is necessary to provide the related App features, respond to deletion requests, comply with applicable law or resolve security and operational issues. Technical and support records can have different retention periods depending on the infrastructure and purpose. We delete or de-identify data when it is no longer needed and no lawful basis for continued retention applies.

To request deletion of data related to your anonymous profile, email owner@great-west-nine.pro with the subject "Heartora Data Deletion." We may need information sufficient to identify the correct anonymous profile before deleting it. Because Heartora does not require an email-linked account, we may not be able to reliably locate or verify every profile using an email address alone.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may be entitled to request access to, correction, deletion or a copy of personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent and to complain to your local privacy regulator.

Contact owner@great-west-nine.pro with the subject "Heartora Privacy Request." Please describe your request and, if possible, include information that lets us locate the anonymous profile involved without unnecessarily sharing sensitive information. We may request reasonable verification before taking action and will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Children and age requirements

Heartora is intended for adults aged 18 or older. The Services are not directed to children, and individuals under 18 should not use them.

If you believe a minor has provided information to Heartora, contact owner@great-west-nine.pro. Please also review our Children's Safety Policy.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information used by the Services from unauthorized access, alteration, loss or disclosure. Internet transmission and electronic storage carry risks, so no security method can be guaranteed to prevent every incident.

Website technology and external links

The Heartora website is primarily an informational website. It has no built-in account registration or payment form. Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request logs and browser information necessary to deliver and protect the website. Some pages may load Google Fonts, which can involve requests to Google's servers.

Links to external websites are subject to the privacy practices of their operators.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this Policy as Heartora changes or legal requirements evolve. The current version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. We will provide additional notice or obtain permission when applicable law requires it.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests to access or delete data, or concerns about this Policy, please email owner@great-west-nine.pro. You can also visit our Contact Us page.
