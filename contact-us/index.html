Frequently asked questions.

Are Heartora characters real people?

No. Heartora features fictional AI characters. Replies are generated by AI, not sent by human operators acting as the characters.

How can I ask about a coin purchase?

Email us with the purchase date, the product or coin amount, and a transaction reference if available. Do not share payment card details. App Store transactions are subject to Apple's purchase and refund systems.

How can I request access to or deletion of my data?

Send your request to owner@great-west-nine.pro with “Heartora Privacy Request” in the subject. We will assess your request in accordance with applicable law.

How do I report a safety concern?

Contact our email address and briefly describe the concern. For issues involving minors, see our Children's Safety page. Do not email illegal imagery.
