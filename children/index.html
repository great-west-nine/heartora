Who can use Heartora

Heartora: AI Romantic Stories ("Heartora," "we," "us," or "our") is an interactive romantic storytelling and entertainment application intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older.

The Heartora mobile application and its related website (together, the "Services") are not designed for or directed to children. Individuals under the age of 18 must not use the Services.

Heartora features fictional romantic scenarios, interactive dates, choice-driven dialogues, relationship progression mechanics, multiple story endings, and AI-powered character conversations.

All romantic relationships and character interactions within Heartora are fictional and simulated for entertainment purposes. Heartora is not a matchmaking service and does not facilitate real-world dating between users.

Our child-safety commitment

Heartora is committed to protecting children from sexual exploitation, abuse, and harmful content. We do not permit the use of our Services to create, promote, facilitate, or distribute content involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.

The following are strictly prohibited, whether the content is real, synthetic, fictional, illustrated, or AI-generated:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexualized depiction of a minor.
  • Sexual or romantic exploitation of minors.
  • Requests to generate, alter, obtain, or distribute sexual content involving minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, coercion, sextortion, or trafficking involving minors.
  • Attempts to generate prohibited content by manipulating character ages or fictional scenarios.
  • Attempts to bypass safety restrictions to obtain prohibited content involving minors.
  • Attempts to use the Services to facilitate abuse, exploitation, or harm to children.

These standards apply to all Heartora features, including interactive romantic stories, fictional dates, dialogue choices, AI-powered conversations, character images, and related support communications.

Interactive stories and AI safety

Heartora provides fictional, choice-driven romantic stories in which users can explore different conversations, relationship outcomes, and story endings.

Characters involved in romantic scenarios must be represented as adults aged 18 or older. Romantic scenarios involving minors are not permitted.

Relationship attributes, including Romance, Trust, and Connection, are fictional gameplay mechanics. They do not represent real relationships, psychological assessments, or measurements of real-world romantic compatibility.

AI-powered conversations and AI-generated character images are also subject to our child-safety standards. Users must not use these features to request, generate, or promote prohibited content involving minors.

AI-generated content may occasionally produce unexpected or inappropriate results. Users are encouraged to report any content that violates these standards.

Heartora does not permit fictional or AI-generated content to be used as a means of circumventing child protection requirements.

How to report a child-safety concern

If you encounter content, a story scenario, a character interaction, an AI-generated response, or other activity that may endanger a child or violate this policy, please contact:

owner@great-west-nine.pro

Please include “Heartora Child Safety Report” in the subject line.

When submitting a report, you may include:

  • A description of the safety concern.
  • The relevant character or story name.
  • A description of the interaction or AI-generated response.
  • The approximate date and time of the incident.
  • Any lawful identifiers or references that may help us locate the reported content.

Do not attach, download, forward, reproduce, or distribute suspected child sexual abuse material.

If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. You may also report suspected online child sexual exploitation directly to the appropriate national authority or reporting hotline.

How reports are handled

We assess child-safety reports received through our support channel and take appropriate action based on the information available, the capabilities of the Services, and applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, appropriate measures may include:

  • Reviewing reported stories, character interactions, or AI-generated content.
  • Removing or restricting access to prohibited content.
  • Restricting or suspending access to the Services where technically possible and appropriate.
  • Reviewing and improving relevant safety measures.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally required.
  • Reporting confirmed or suspected illegal activity to appropriate authorities where required or appropriate under applicable law.

We cannot guarantee that every report will result in a particular action, but child-safety reports are treated as serious matters.

For parents and guardians

Heartora is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. Parents and guardians are encouraged to use available device-level parental controls and age-based restrictions to prevent minors from accessing age-inappropriate applications.

If you believe that a person under the age of 18 is using Heartora or has submitted personal information to the Services, please contact our support address.

We can review the report and address requests concerning access to the Services and the deletion of personal information in accordance with applicable law.

For additional information about data collection, processing, retention, and deletion, please review our Privacy Policy.
