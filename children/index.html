Children’s Safety

Who can use Heartora

Heartora is an AI character chat and entertainment service intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not designed for or directed to children. People under 18 should not use Heartora, interact with its AI characters, or purchase digital content through the App.

AI characters in Heartora are fictional, and AI-generated interactions are not conversations with real people.

Our child-safety commitment

We do not permit the sexual exploitation or abuse of children in connection with the Services. The following are prohibited, whether a description, conversation, prompt, image, or other content is real, synthetic, fictional, or AI-generated:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexualized depiction of a minor.
  • Requests to generate, alter, obtain, or distribute sexual content involving minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, coercion, sextortion, or trafficking involving minors.
  • Attempts to use the Services to facilitate abuse, exploitation, or harm to children.

This standard applies across the App, its AI features, and related support communications.

How to report a child-safety concern

If you see content or activity that may endanger a child or violate this policy, contact owner@great-west-nine.pro and include “Heartora Child Safety Report” in the subject line.

Provide relevant details such as the nature of the concern, a description of the interaction, the approximate date, and any non-illegal identifiers or references that may help locate it. Do not attach, download, forward, or distribute suspected child sexual abuse images.

If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. You may also report suspected online child sexual exploitation directly to the appropriate national authority or reporting hotline.

How reports are handled

We assess reports received through our support channel and take appropriate action based on the information available, the capabilities of the Services, and applicable law. Measures may include restricting access to offending content or accounts, preserving relevant information where legally required, and notifying appropriate authorities when required or appropriate.

We cannot guarantee that every report will result in a particular action, but child-safety reports are treated as serious matters.

For parents and guardians

If you believe a person under 18 is using Heartora or has submitted personal information to the Services, please contact our support address. We can review the report and address personal-data requests in accordance with applicable law.

For information about the categories of data that may be processed, please review the Privacy Policy.

Dedicated contact

Child safety reports and related inquiries: owner@great-west-nine.pro.
